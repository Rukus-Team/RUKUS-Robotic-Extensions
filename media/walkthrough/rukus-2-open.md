# Open a Cluster as a Workspace

When RUKUS is found, its clusters appear in the **RUKUS Clusters** view. Clicking one opens its backup folder as the workspace and writes the cluster's robots into the cell's `cell.json` - names, addresses and devices. FTP passwords move into VS Code's secret storage.

**You should see** the window switch to the cluster's backup folder, its robots in the **Cell** view, and a `.robocode-cell/cell.json` in the workspace.
