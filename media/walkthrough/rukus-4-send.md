# Send a Cell to RUKUS

**Send Cell to RUKUS** writes the open cell's controllers to RUKUS as a cluster, or updates the cluster it came from. RUKUS's own safety copy is made first, and RUKUS-only fields ride along untouched.

A cell made before RUKUS was installed is remembered and offered the next time RUKUS turns up.

**You should see** a confirmation that the cell's controllers were written to the cluster.

[Send Cell to RUKUS…](command:robotCode.rukus.exportCell)
